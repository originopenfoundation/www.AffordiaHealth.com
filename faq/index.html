Frequently Asked Questions

01

Do I have to pay for everything in advance?

No. Affordia Health operates primarily on a pay-as-you-go service model.

02

Is the initial consultation free?

Yes. Your initial consultation with Affordia Health is free and does not obligate you to purchase any service.

03

Do I have to purchase a complete medical travel package?

No. You choose the services you need.

04

Who pays the hospital?

Wherever practicable, you pay the hospital or healthcare provider directly.

05

Are you a hospital or medical provider?

No. Affordia Health is an independent service and coordination company.

06

Can I choose the hospital myself?

Yes. You remain in control of your medical journey.

07

Will someone help me when I arrive in China?

If local assistance is included in the services you select, yes.

08

Do I need to speak Chinese?

Not necessarily. Interpretation and communication assistance can be included in your Affordia Health services.

More about your request

Where can Affordia Health help?

Our current local coordination focus is Shenyang, China. Ask us about your individual request; availability and scope must be confirmed.

Can I contact you from outside China?

Yes. International customers can contact us from abroad. You do not need to travel to begin an introductory conversation.

Can you help me explore hospitals in Shenyang?

You can request help researching options. Departments, expertise, availability and conditions vary; the provider confirms its services.

Do you make medical decisions?

No. Qualified independent clinicians and healthcare providers make clinical decisions and provide treatment.

Can I ask about non-medical assistance?

Yes. You may ask about interpretation, travel, accommodation or other practical requests in Shenyang. Each request is assessed individually.

How are fees and provider payments handled?

We agree the scope and any consulting or coordination fee before work begins. Customers normally pay third-party providers directly; any exception is disclosed before agreement.

Can you guarantee an appointment or outcome?

No. Providers decide whether they can accept a request. Neither appointments nor clinical outcomes can be guaranteed.

Can I use insurance?

Insurance coverage and payment depend on your policy and provider. Please verify the conditions directly with the insurer and relevant provider.

What happens after I write to you?

We discuss your requirements and whether assistance may be possible. Do not send medical records or sensitive health details in your first general inquiry.

Are services in Turkey currently available?

Turkey and other regions may be considered for future expansion. They should not be assumed to be currently available.

What is Affordia Health?

We provide independent consulting and service coordination. We are not a hospital, healthcare provider or insurer.

Where is the business based?

Our listed postal address is in Nedožery-Brezany, Slovakia. Our current local coordination focus is Shenyang, China.

Can you help coordinate a medical appointment?

You can ask us to research and coordinate an appointment. Availability and acceptance must be confirmed by the provider.

Can I compare options and ask about costs?

You can ask us to research available options and their practical conditions. Providers confirm their own services and fees.

What does Grade III Class A mean?

It is a Chinese hospital classification. Classification alone does not establish that a specific department is suitable for your needs.

Is the free initial consultation a medical appointment?

No. It is an introductory discussion with Affordia Health about your request, not a medical consultation.

Are WhatsApp and WeChat available?

Please use the email contact shown on this website. A verified direct messaging identifier has not been published.

How quickly will I receive a response?

No response-time guarantee is currently stated. Please contact us by email to discuss your request.

What if you cannot arrange a service?

Each request is assessed individually. If we cannot assist, we will discuss the situation rather than promise a result.

Can an agency or business discuss cooperation?

Yes. Businesses and agencies may contact us about potential cooperation. A discussion does not imply an existing partnership.

Can you help outside Shenyang?

Our current local focus is Shenyang. Please ask about other locations; service outside the current area is not guaranteed.

How are options selected?

We discuss your priorities and research possible providers. Suitability, scope, costs and availability must be confirmed with the relevant provider.

Can you make a referral?

You may ask about another provider. Any introduction depends on the request and the provider; a referral or acceptance is not guaranteed.

Can services be coordinated while I stay abroad?

You can ask about remote coordination. We assess what is feasible for your individual request.

How is my personal information handled?

Please read the privacy notice linked on this website and avoid sending medical records or sensitive health information in your first inquiry.

How do I start?

Email affordiahealth@gmail.com and describe what assistance you need, your country and how you would like us to contact you.

What services can I ask about?

You may ask about healthcare coordination, travel, interpretation and other practical local assistance. The scope is agreed individually and availability must be confirmed.

Can I contact you if I am unsure what I need?

Yes. Describe your situation in general terms and we can discuss the next steps without requiring medical terminology.
