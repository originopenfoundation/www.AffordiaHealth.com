Aftercare & Recovery Treatment Is Not the End of the Journey A healthcare journey does not necessarily end when a procedure is completed or when a client leaves the hospital.

Recovery can be one of the most important stages of the entire process.

Depending on the treatment, recovery may involve:

follow-up consultations, rehabilitation, physiotherapy, wound or mobility management, medication monitoring, rest, nutrition, gradual return to activity, additional examinations, or longer-term lifestyle changes.

Affordia Health can help clients coordinate the practical parts of this stage.

Treatment Solves the Immediate Problem.

Recovery Builds the Next Step.

Recovery Starts Before You Leave the Hospital Before leaving the healthcare provider, clients should understand the relevant discharge and follow-up instructions given by their medical team.

Depending on the case, this may include:

medication instructions, physical restrictions, wound care, follow-up appointments, warning signs, rehabilitation recommendations, dietary instructions, and return-to-activity guidance.

These medical instructions come from the treating healthcare professionals.

Affordia Health can assist with understanding the practical journey around them.

Affordia Does Not Replace Postoperative Medical Care Affordia Health does not independently determine:

how a wound should be treated, which medication should be used, when a client should stop medication, when exercise should begin, whether a complication is developing, or whether further medical treatment is necessary.

These are healthcare decisions.

They remain with qualified healthcare professionals.

Our role is to help connect the client with the appropriate services and coordinate the practical elements of recovery.

Follow-Up Coordination After treatment, a client may need further contact with the hospital or specialist.

Affordia Health can help coordinate:

follow-up appointments, online follow-up consultations, communication with the treating provider, additional examinations, medical document coordination, and other agreed follow-up services.

Where the client has already returned home, follow-up may sometimes be coordinated remotely where clinically appropriate.

Recovery in China Some clients may need or prefer to remain in China for a period after treatment.

Depending on the selected services and healthcare recommendations, Affordia Health may assist with:

suitable accommodation, local transportation, hospital follow-up visits, interpretation, practical daily assistance, rehabilitation coordination, and preparation for the return journey.

The required level of support depends on the individual case.

Returning Home Returning home should be planned according to the client's medical condition and professional medical advice.

The treating healthcare provider should determine whether the client is medically fit to travel.

Affordia Health may assist with the practical preparation of the return journey but does not independently determine medical fitness to fly or travel.

Medical Clearance Comes From Healthcare Professionals.

Recovery Does Not Have to Continue in China A major advantage of the Affordia Health network is that recovery services do not necessarily need to be provided in the same country where treatment occurred.

For example:

Treatment in China ↓ Return to Europe ↓ Rehabilitation in Slovakia ↓ Physiotherapy Near Home ↓ Online Follow-Up ↓ Affordia Care This can allow the client to combine specialised treatment abroad with practical long-term recovery closer to home.

Rehabilitation Some procedures require structured rehabilitation.

Affordia Health can help identify and coordinate suitable rehabilitation providers within the developing Affordia Healthcare Partner network.

Depending on the client's needs, rehabilitation may include:

physiotherapy, mobility programmes, musculoskeletal rehabilitation, neurological rehabilitation, postoperative rehabilitation, cardiac rehabilitation, respiratory rehabilitation, or other professionally supervised programmes.

The appropriate rehabilitation plan must be determined by qualified healthcare professionals.

Medical Spas and Thermal Recovery For suitable clients, recovery may also include professionally supervised medical spa or thermal programmes.

Affordia Health is developing relationships with healthcare, rehabilitation and thermal spa providers, including potential partners in Slovakia and other European countries.

These services may be relevant for:

mobility recovery, musculoskeletal rehabilitation, physical conditioning, post-treatment recovery, longer-term rehabilitation, or preventive health programmes, where professionally appropriate.

A medical spa should complement appropriate healthcare — not replace medically necessary treatment.

Recovery Close to Home Travelling abroad may make sense for a specialised procedure.

Long-term rehabilitation may make more sense close to home.

Affordia can therefore help explore both international and local recovery options.

The objective is not to keep the client travelling unnecessarily.

It is to find a practical recovery pathway.

Treatment May Be International. Recovery Can Be Local.

Second Opinion After Treatment Sometimes a client wants another professional perspective after a procedure or major course of treatment.

Affordia Health can help coordinate an appropriate second opinion where available.

This may be useful when:

symptoms persist, recovery is slower than expected, the client wants another perspective, additional treatment has been recommended, or the client wants to better understand the next stage of care.

The medical opinion itself is provided by the relevant qualified professional.

Online Follow-Up Where appropriate, some follow-up consultations may be possible online.

Affordia Health can help coordinate access to:

the original healthcare provider, another specialist, a rehabilitation professional, a Chinese healthcare professional, or another appropriate provider.

Online consultation does not replace physical examination when one is medically necessary.

Recovery Is Individual Two people undergoing the same procedure may recover differently.

Recovery may depend on:

age, overall health, type of procedure, medical complexity, physical condition, existing illnesses, rehabilitation, sleep, nutrition, stress, and other individual factors.

For this reason, Affordia Health does not promise fixed recovery times.

Recovery Should Follow the Individual — Not a Generic Timeline.

Warning Signs Require Medical Attention Affordia Health is not an emergency medical service.

If a client experiences concerning symptoms during recovery, they should contact an appropriate healthcare professional or emergency service.

Depending on the procedure, warning signs may require urgent professional assessment.

Affordia local assistance may help with practical coordination where available, but it should never delay necessary emergency or medical care.

Long-Term Prevention After Treatment Recovery can also be an opportunity to reconsider longterm health.

Some medical problems involve risk factors that may continue after treatment.

Depending on the condition, qualified professionals may recommend changes concerning:

physical activity, body weight, nutrition, sleep, stress, smoking, alcohol, work habits, rehabilitation, or other modifiable factors.

Not every condition is caused by lifestyle and not every recurrence can be prevented.

But where relevant risk factors can be changed, treatment may be followed by a longer-term prevention strategy.

From Recovery to Affordia Care Clients who want continued long-term support may transition into Affordia Care.

Affordia Care is designed around:

prevention, health understanding, online consultation, lifestyle support, monitoring, professional referrals, and long-term health planning.

This creates a continuous pathway:

Treatment ↓ Recovery ↓ Rehabilitation ↓ Follow-Up ↓ Prevention ↓ Affordia Care Your Healthcare Journey Can Continue Some clients will need Affordia only for one service.

Others may want support throughout several stages of their healthcare journey.

Both are possible.

You remain free to decide which services you need.

There is no obligation to continue using Affordia after treatment.

If further support creates value for you, it can be added when needed.

Continue Only When It Helps You.

Coordination Across Providers Recovery may involve more than one organisation.

For example:

hospital, specialist, physiotherapist, rehabilitation centre, medical spa, local doctor, insurance professional, and Affordia Care.

Affordia can help coordinate the practical connections between these services where agreed.

Each professional remains responsible for their own service.

Recovery Costs Recovery services are not automatically included in the cost of treatment or other Affordia Health services.

Where additional Affordia assistance is required, services and fees are agreed separately according to our modular and pay-as-you-go model.

Healthcare and rehabilitation providers may also charge their own fees directly.

Clients remain in control of which services they choose.

Recovery Planning Where appropriate, recovery planning can begin before the client travels for treatment.

This may help answer practical questions such as:

How long might I need to stay after treatment?

Will I need assistance during the first days?

Can I travel home immediately?

Will I need rehabilitation?

Where can rehabilitation take place?

Will I need follow-up appointments?

Who will I contact after returning home?

The medical answers must come from appropriate healthcare professionals.

Affordia can help turn those answers into a practical plan.

Complete the Journey Properly Successful healthcare is not only about reaching the operating room.

It is also about what happens afterward.

Treatment is one stage.

Recovery is another.

Prevention may be the next.

Affordia Health is designed to help clients connect these stages when support is needed.
