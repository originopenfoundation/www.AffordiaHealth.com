Healthcare Partners Connect Your Healthcare Services With the Affordia Network Affordia Health is building a network of healthcare and care providers capable of offering clients more choices — locally and internationally.

Our objective is simple:

Understand what the client needs and connect that need with an appropriate healthcare solution.

China is an important part of the Affordia Health network, but healthcare does not always require international travel.

Sometimes the best solution may be in another country.

Sometimes it may be in the client's own country.

Affordia Health wants to build access to both.

The Right Care. In the Right Place. For the Right Client.

Who Can Become a Healthcare Partner?

Affordia Health is interested in cooperation with established providers across healthcare, recovery, prevention and related care.

Potential partners include:

Hospitals Private Clinics Specialist Medical Centres Diagnostic Centres Rehabilitation Centres Physiotherapy Providers Thermal Spas and Medical Spas Recovery and Convalescence Facilities Preventive Healthcare Centres Traditional Chinese Medicine Providers Mental Health and Wellbeing Providers Dental Clinics Vision and Eye-Care Centres Veterinary Hospitals and Specialist Veterinary Clinics and other qualified providers capable of solving a genuine client need.

Local Healthcare Matters Too Affordia Health is not designed around sending every client abroad.

If an appropriate solution exists locally, it should be considered.

A client living in Slovakia may benefit from a Slovak healthcare provider.

A Swiss client may find an appropriate rehabilitation programme elsewhere in Europe.

Another client may need highly specialised treatment in China.

Destination follows the need — the need should not follow the destination.

Slovak Healthcare & Thermal Spa Partners Slovakia has an established tradition of thermal spas, rehabilitation and therapeutic stays.

These capabilities can become part of the Affordia network alongside conventional healthcare services.

Potential cooperation may include:

medical spa programmes, postoperative recovery, musculoskeletal rehabilitation, physiotherapy, mobility programmes, recovery stays, preventive programmes, and other professionally supervised services.

These services can be relevant both to international clients visiting Slovakia and to Slovak residents looking for appropriate care within their own country.

A global healthcare network should also create value locally.

Rehabilitation and Recovery Treatment does not necessarily end when a client leaves the hospital.

Recovery may require weeks or months.

Affordia Health therefore wants to connect treatment with appropriate post-treatment services where needed.

A journey could potentially continue through:

Treatment ↓ Discharge ↓ Recovery ↓ Rehabilitation ↓ Physiotherapy ↓ Prevention ↓ Long-Term Care A rehabilitation provider does not need to perform the original treatment to become an important part of the client's healthcare journey.

Before and After Treatment Healthcare partners can participate at different stages.

Before Treatment Diagnostics Preventive programmes Specialist consultations Second opinions Physical preparation Treatment Hospitals Specialist clinics Surgical centres Dental care Other appropriate clinical services After Treatment Rehabilitation Physiotherapy Recovery programmes Medical spas Convalescence Follow-up services Long-Term Prevention Lifestyle programmes Monitoring Affordia Care A strong network should support more than one moment in a client's life.

You Do Not Need to Provide Everything Affordia Health is built around modular services.

A partner may provide only one highly specialised capability.

That is enough.

A rehabilitation centre should not need to become a medical-tourism company.

A thermal spa should not need to organise international patient logistics.

A specialist clinic should not need to build its own global client-acquisition infrastructure.

Focus on What You Do Best.

Affordia Health can help connect that capability with a wider client journey.

Reach Clients Beyond Your Existing Market Excellent healthcare capacity can remain underused simply because potential clients do not know that it exists.

Affordia Health aims to reduce this gap.

Partnership can create opportunities for:

new domestic clients, international clients, self-pay clients, clients seeking alternatives, post-treatment clients, clients requiring rehabilitation, and clients referred through the wider Affordia network.

For providers, this can create additional demand without requiring them to build an international acquisition system from the beginning.

Domestic Clients Are Welcome Affordia Health is not limited to medical tourists.

A healthcare partner can also receive clients from its own country.

For example:

Slovak Client ↓ Affordia Health ↓ Need Identified ↓ Suitable Slovak Partner ↓ Local Healthcare or Recovery Service No flight.

No international hospital.

No unnecessary complexity.

If the appropriate solution is local, Affordia can help make the local connection.

International Clients Other partners may want to expand internationally.

Affordia Health can help connect appropriate international demand with available healthcare capacity.

Depending on the case and agreed services, the wider Affordia network may assist with:

client communication, international coordination, appointments, travel preparation, airport pickup, accommodation, interpretation, local support, and other components of the healthcare journey.

The healthcare provider can remain focused on healthcare.

Partner Independence Healthcare Partners remain responsible for their own professional services.

Participation in the Affordia network does not automatically require a provider to:

change its brand, become an Affordia company, transfer ownership, or abandon its existing client relationships.

The precise commercial and operational relationship is determined by the applicable partnership arrangement.

Your organisation remains your organisation.

Professional Responsibility Remains Clear Doctors, hospitals, clinics and other regulated healthcare professionals remain responsible for the healthcare they provide.

Affordia Health does not replace professional clinical judgment.

Affordia may coordinate access and related services.

The healthcare provider remains responsible for its professional services.

The client remains responsible for the final healthcare decision.

Clear boundaries protect everyone involved.

Partnership Should Create Economic Value Affordia Health is interested in partnerships that create genuine value for both clients and providers.

Depending on the cooperation model, benefits may include:

Additional Client Volume Reach clients beyond your existing acquisition channels.

New Geographic Markets Make appropriate services visible internationally.

Domestic Referrals Receive relevant clients from within your own country.

Better Capacity Utilisation Connect available capacity with existing demand.

Additional Revenue Generate business from services and capacity that already exist.

Cross-Service Referrals Participate in healthcare journeys involving multiple providers.

Long-Term Client Relationships Become part of treatment, rehabilitation, recovery or prevention pathways rather than isolated transactions.

Affordia Connects Different Capabilities One provider does not need to solve the entire healthcare journey.

A client could potentially receive treatment from one partner and rehabilitation from another.

For example:

Specialist Hospital in China ↓ Affordia Coordination ↓ Client Returns to Europe ↓ Rehabilitation Partner in Slovakia ↓ Affordia Care ↓ Long-Term Prevention Each organisation performs the part it does best.

Affordia helps connect the journey.

More Choice for Clients Building a larger network is not about directing every client toward commercial partners.

It is about increasing the number of realistic options available.

Affordia Health may consider factors such as:

healthcare need, professional capability, specialisation, quality, availability, location, price, client budget, waiting time, recovery requirements, and other relevant circumstances.

Partnership Expands Choice. It Should Never Remove It.

Transparent Commercial Relationships Affordia Health believes clients should understand who is providing their service.

Where relevant, commercial relationships should be disclosed appropriately.

Healthcare recommendations should not be presented as independent when a material commercial relationship requires disclosure.

Long-term trust is more valuable than one referral.

Quality Before Quantity Being listed in a network should mean something.

Potential Healthcare Partners may therefore be evaluated according to relevant factors such as:

professional qualifications, regulatory status, service quality, specialisation, facilities, client experience, communication, pricing transparency, reliability, international-client capability, and operational capacity.

Different types of providers require different evaluation criteria.

A hospital should not be evaluated in the same way as a thermal spa or transportation provider.

One Network. Different Healthcare Journeys.

Affordia Health can support very different client pathways.

International Treatment Client → Affordia → Healthcare Provider Abroad Local Healthcare Client → Affordia → Healthcare Provider in Their Own Country Recovery Treatment → Affordia → Rehabilitation / Spa / Recovery Partner Long-Term Care Treatment → Recovery → Affordia Care → Prevention Veterinary Healthcare Pet Owner → Affordia → Specialist Veterinary Provider The common element is not geography.

The common element is solving the client's need.
