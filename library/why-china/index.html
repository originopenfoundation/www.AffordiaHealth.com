Why China?

Affordable Healthcare Solutions Worldwide.

A Different Perspective on Global Healthcare When people think about travelling abroad for healthcare, China may not always be the first destination that comes to mind.

We believe that is changing.

China combines one of the world's largest healthcare systems with major tertiary hospitals, highly specialised medical departments, extensive clinical experience and rapidly developing medical technology.

For international patients looking for another healthcare option, China deserves serious consideration.

Not simply because healthcare can be more affordable — but because affordability can exist alongside scale, technology, expertise and access.

A Healthcare System Built at Extraordinary Scale China provides healthcare for a population of more than one billion people.

That scale creates something that smaller healthcare systems cannot easily reproduce: enormous clinical volume.

Major Chinese hospitals and specialist departments can treat very large numbers of patients and perform substantial numbers of procedures.

For certain specialists and medical teams, this can mean extensive practical experience accumulated through repeated exposure to particular conditions and procedures.

For an international patient, the important question is therefore not simply:

Which hospital is the biggest?

It is:

Which hospital, department and medical team has the right experience for my particular case?

That is one of the questions Affordia Health helps clients explore.

Grade 3A Hospitals For hospital-based treatment, Affordia Health primarily focuses on China's Grade 3A (Tier 3A ) hospitals.

China operates a tiered hospital system, with tertiary hospitals positioned at the highest level and responsible particularly for complex, serious and difficult medical conditions.

China also applies national assessment standards to tertiary hospitals covering areas including medical quality, safety and hospital management.

But we do not believe a classification should be the end of the selection process.

Not every leading hospital is equally strong in every medical field.

One hospital may have an outstanding cardiovascular department.

Another may be particularly experienced in orthopaedics.

Another may have highly specialised oncology, neurosurgery or another clinical department.

Affordia Health therefore looks beyond the hospital classification and considers the speciality and department relevant to the individual case.

Advanced Medical Technology China is no longer simply a low-cost manufacturing economy.

It is one of the world's major technology and innovation centres — and healthcare is part of that development.

Leading Chinese hospitals increasingly operate sophisticated diagnostic, surgical, imaging and treatment infrastructure.

China's national healthcare strategy explicitly emphasises medical technology innovation, clinical research, specialised medical centres and the development of highlevel hospitals.

For patients considering treatment abroad, this creates an important combination:

Advanced infrastructure + experienced medical teams + large clinical volumes.

Experience Through Scale Medical technology matters.

But technology alone does not treat patients.

People do.

China's population and the scale of its healthcare system mean that physicians working in major specialist centres may encounter large numbers of cases throughout their careers.

In appropriate specialties, this can create significant accumulated clinical experience.

This is particularly relevant when selecting a hospital for a specialised procedure.

Affordia Health therefore does not simply ask whether a hospital offers a procedure.

We look for the most appropriate available combination of:

Hospital → Department → Specialisation → Experience → Availability → Cost → Location → Support Potentially Faster Access Waiting for healthcare can be frustrating — particularly when a person already knows that treatment may be necessary.

One reason international patients may consider China is the possibility of obtaining faster access to certain specialists, examinations or treatments than they can obtain at home.

Actual availability depends on the hospital, specialist, procedure and individual medical case.

For that reason, Affordia Health does not promise an artificial universal waiting time.

We check availability for the individual client.

When appropriate treatment can be accessed sooner, that difference can become one of the most important reasons for considering healthcare abroad.

Healthcare Designed to Remain Accessible China's healthcare system has a strong public component.

Government policy places significant emphasis on accessibility, public hospitals, medical insurance coverage and the availability of healthcare across different levels of the system.

Public hospitals remain central to healthcare delivery, while tertiary hospitals concentrate increasingly on serious, complex and difficult medical conditions.

China's basic medical insurance system covered approximately 95% of the population at the end of 2025.

The scale of public healthcare and government involvement creates a healthcare environment fundamentally different from purely private international medical markets.

International Patients Can Access Public Hospitals An important and sometimes overlooked feature of the Chinese healthcare system is that foreigners can receive treatment in Chinese public hospitals.

This creates another option beyond expensive international private hospitals.

Affordia Health helps international clients explore suitable healthcare possibilities while considering the level of hospital, relevant department, practical accessibility and available international-patient support.

Where appropriate, international or VIP services may also be considered.

The right choice depends on the individual client.

Affordable Healthcare Without Building the Journey Around Price Alone Affordability is important to us.

It is part of our name.

But Affordia Health does not believe the cheapest healthcare is automatically the best healthcare.

Treatment costs can vary significantly depending on:

the procedure, medical complexity, hospital, department, doctor, technology required, length of hospitalisation, medication, and level of additional service.

For some treatments, China may provide a substantial cost advantage compared with certain Western healthcare markets.

For others, the difference may be smaller.

That is why we prefer real information over universal percentage claims.

Where reliable information is available, we aim to help clients understand expected costs before they make their decision.

Affordable should mean better access to appropriate healthcare — not simply finding the lowest price.

Western Medicine and Traditional Chinese Medicine China also offers something unusual within global healthcare.

Modern Western medicine and Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) exist within the same broader healthcare system.

International patients may therefore have access to conventional medical treatment while also having the possibility of exploring TCM services where appropriate and desired.

Affordia Health can also help arrange consultations with qualified Chinese medical professionals, including TCM practitioners where available.

These are separate healthcare options, and clients remain free to decide what they wish to explore.

A Healthcare System That Continues to Develop China's healthcare system is not standing still.

National policy continues to focus on strengthening tertiary hospitals, specialist medical centres, regional medical capacity, clinical research, healthcare technology and access to care.

The country is simultaneously investing heavily in areas such as artificial intelligence, robotics, advanced manufacturing and digital infrastructure.

We believe the interaction between these capabilities and healthcare will continue to create new medical possibilities.

China is interesting as a healthcare destination today — and its potential may be even greater tomorrow.
