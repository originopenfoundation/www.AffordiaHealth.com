Our Services Your Journey. Your Choice.

Every medical journey is different.

Some clients only need help finding the right hospital.

Others need assistance with appointments, travel, accommodation, interpretation and local support. Some prefer us to coordinate the entire journey.

Affordia Health gives you the flexibility to choose only the services you need.

Start with one service or choose a complete medical journey. Add additional services whenever you need them.

Core Services Medical Case Review Tell us what you need and let us understand your situation.

We review the information you provide, identify your requirements and determine how Affordia Health may be able to assist.

Where medical evaluation is necessary, medical information is referred to an appropriate healthcare provider or qualified medical professional.

Your initial consultation with Affordia Health is free.

Hospital & Clinic Selection Finding a hospital is easy.

Finding the right hospital and the right department for a particular case can be much more difficult.

Affordia Health researches suitable healthcare providers according to your individual requirements, medical speciality, available expertise, timeframe, budget, location and accessibility.

For hospital-based treatment, our primary focus is on Grade 3A (Tier 3A ) hospitals.

We also consider the relevant department and specialisation because even among leading hospitals, expertise can differ significantly.

We start with your needs — not with a predetermined hospital.

Medical Coordination Once you have selected a healthcare option, we can help coordinate the practical process with the healthcare provider.

Depending on your requirements, this may include communication with the hospital, transfer of relevant documentation, appointment coordination, treatment scheduling and assistance obtaining available information before your arrival.

Medical diagnosis, treatment recommendations and clinical decisions remain exclusively with the relevant healthcare professionals.

Travel Assistance We help you prepare the practical side of your journey to China.

This may include guidance concerning your itinerary, arrival arrangements, transportation, accommodation and coordination between your travel schedule and healthcare appointments.

You remain free to purchase and manage your own flights.

Visa Assistance Where a visa is required, we can assist with preparation and help coordinate relevant supporting information available for your journey.

You remain the applicant and submit your visa application directly through the appropriate official process.

Visa approval remains exclusively the decision of the competent authorities.

Airport Pickup Arriving in an unfamiliar country should not be the difficult part of your medical journey.

Where available, we can arrange airport pickup and transportation to your hotel, hospital or other agreed destination.

Our objective is to make sure you know what happens after you land.

Accommodation Assistance We can help identify and arrange suitable accommodation according to your location, treatment schedule, length of stay and budget.

Where appropriate, accommodation can be selected with convenient access to the hospital or treatment facility.

You decide which accommodation option you prefer.

Interpreter Service Language should not become an unnecessary barrier during your medical journey.

Interpreter assistance can be arranged for hospital visits, appointments and other situations where communication support is required.

The level of interpretation support can be adapted to the individual journey.

Local Support Our local presence is one of the most important parts of the Affordia Health service.

Depending on the services selected and the region in which your journey takes place, our team can assist with practical issues during your stay in China.

This may include transportation coordination, hospital navigation, communication assistance, appointmentrelated questions and unexpected logistical situations.

When you need practical help in China, you have someone to contact.

Complete Medical Journey For clients who prefer comprehensive support, Affordia Health can coordinate the complete medical journey.

Depending on your individual requirements, this can combine:

Hospital selection → Medical coordination → Travel preparation → Visa assistance → Accommodation → Airport pickup → Local transportation → Interpretation → Hospital assistance → Local support → Return preparation → Follow-up coordination.

The exact journey is built around your needs.

One journey. One point of coordination. Support from preparation to return.

Additional Services Additional services can be selected individually or added to your medical journey according to your requirements.

Second Medical Opinion We can help coordinate access to an appropriate qualified medical professional in China for an independent second medical opinion.

The medical opinion itself is provided by the relevant doctor or healthcare institution, not by Affordia Health.

Online Consultation with Chinese Specialists Not every healthcare journey needs to begin with a flight.

Where available, we can help arrange an online consultation with an appropriate healthcare professional in China.

This may help clients obtain additional professional input before deciding whether travelling to China is appropriate for them.

Traditional Chinese Medicine Consultation We can help arrange consultations with qualified practitioners of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM).

Depending on availability and the client's requirements, consultations may be organised online or as part of a healthcare journey in China.

Follow-Up Coordination Your journey does not necessarily end when you return home.

Where appropriate, we can help coordinate follow-up communication, additional appointments, online consultations or further contact with the healthcare provider.

Medical Document Assistance International healthcare frequently requires documents to move between different languages and healthcare systems.

We can assist with organising medical documentation and, where required, arranging appropriate translation services.

Rehabilitation Coordination Where rehabilitation is required following treatment or surgery, we can help research and coordinate suitable rehabilitation options available in China.

Family & Companion Assistance You may not be travelling alone.

We can help coordinate practical arrangements for accompanying family members or companions, including accommodation, transportation and other travel-related requirements.

Veterinary Medical Services Access to specialised healthcare abroad does not have to be limited to people.

Where appropriate services are available, Affordia Health can assist owners seeking veterinary healthcare in China.

Services may include veterinary hospital selection, specialist or surgical coordination, communication assistance and practical journey support.

Veterinary treatment is provided directly by qualified veterinary professionals and veterinary healthcare institutions.

Emergency Local Assistance Unexpected practical situations can occur during international travel.

For Affordia Health clients travelling within supported regions, additional local assistance may be available when an unexpected logistical problem affects their medical journey.

This service does not replace medical emergency services, travel insurance, consular assistance or public emergency services.

Flexible by Design You do not have to purchase services you do not need.

Perhaps you already have accommodation but need hospital access.

Perhaps you have selected a hospital but need interpretation and local assistance.

Perhaps you only want a second medical opinion.

Or perhaps you want someone to coordinate the entire journey.

With Affordia Health, you choose the level of support that works for you.

Clear and Separate Payments Our service fees are separate from the cost of your medical treatment.

Wherever practicable:

You pay hospitals and healthcare providers directly for healthcare.

You pay Affordia Health for the Affordia Health services you choose.

Flights and other third-party expenses can also remain under your direct control unless a different arrangement is specifically agreed.
