Partner Network Build With Affordia Health Bring the clients. Build the relationship. We provide the China infrastructure.

Affordia Health is building an international partner network connecting people and independent businesses around the world with one coordinated healthcare access and support infrastructure in China.

You do not need to open an Affordia Health office.

You do not need to operate under the Affordia Health brand.

You do not even need to own an existing medical travel business to start.

There are different ways to participate.

From introducing an occasional client to building your own independent healthcare travel business, Affordia Health provides a pathway for partners who can connect people with healthcare opportunities in China.

One Network. Different Ways to Participate.

Not every partner wants to build the same business.

Some people simply know someone who may need our services.

Others can regularly introduce clients.

Some want to build their own client portfolio.

Others already operate an established business and want access to reliable infrastructure in China.

Affordia Health is designed to accommodate different levels of participation.

Referral Partner Make an Introduction The simplest way to participate.

A Referral Partner introduces potential new clients to Affordia Health.

You do not need your own website.

You do not need to manage the client's journey.

You do not need to build relationships with hospitals in China.

You make the connection.

Affordia Health takes care of the process from there.

Eligible successful referrals are rewarded according to the applicable Affordia Health Referral Program.

One introduction can be enough to start.

Independent Partner Build Your Own Client Base Independent Partners can take a more active role.

You can find your own clients, develop relationships with them, communicate with them and build your own network.

You may operate under your own name or business identity.

You do not have to become an Affordia Health-branded operation.

When your client needs healthcare access and journey support in China, the case can connect to the Affordia Health central system and China operations.

Your clients. Your relationships. Our China infrastructure.

Business Partner Connect Your Existing Business Already operating a business with access to potential clients?

Affordia Health can provide the China-side infrastructure without requiring you to abandon your existing brand.

Potential partners may include businesses operating in areas such as international services, travel, healthcare coordination, international communities or other relevant client networks.

The business remains independent.

Affordia Health provides the agreed China-side services.

This creates a simple proposition:

Keep your business. Keep your brand. Expand what you can offer.

Country Partner Develop a Market For partners interested in developing a larger operation, a Country Partner model may be available.

Country Partners can systematically develop clients within an agreed market and build a long-term local operation connected to the Affordia Health network.

Depending on the agreement, they may operate under their own brand or an approved Affordia Health identity.

Country Partners can develop their own:

marketing strategy, client acquisition channels, local communication, client relationships, business development, and growth strategy.

The China operational side remains connected to the central Affordia Health infrastructure.

Build relationships.

Understand the market.

Support the client relationship.

Affordia Health Central System Coordinates the network.

Maintains service principles.

Supports transparent workflows.

China Operations Researches appropriate available healthcare access.

Coordinates healthcare providers.

Supports the client's journey in China.

Healthcare Providers Doctors and healthcare institutions remain responsible for medical assessment, diagnosis, treatment and clinical care.

Everyone focuses on what they do best.

One China Infrastructure Building reliable operations in another country takes time.

Hospital access.

Local communication.

Transportation.

Interpreters.

Accommodation knowledge.

Regional experience.

Problem solving.

On-the-ground support.

Affordia Health partners do not need to recreate this infrastructure independently.

Cases can connect to one coordinated China operational network.

Many Sources of Clients.

One China Infrastructure.

That is the foundation of the Affordia Health Partner Network.

Keep Your Independence Independent partners remain independent.

Affordia Health does not require every participant to use the same local business model.

Different countries require different approaches.

Different communities require different communication.

Different entrepreneurs build businesses differently.

Partners may develop their own strategy, brand, client relationships and commercial activities according to their individual agreement and applicable requirements.

We provide infrastructure, not unnecessary control over how you build your market.

Independence Has Boundaries Independence cannot come at the expense of clients or the network.

Partners participating in the Affordia Health system must not:

mislead clients, make unauthorised medical claims, misrepresent their relationship with Affordia Health, damage the Affordia Health brand, interfere with another partner's established client relationship, create unnecessary internal conflicts, or compromise client transparency.

Partners operating under their own brands must also clearly explain their role where Affordia Health services are involved.

Independent business. Transparent relationship. Clear responsibilities.

Fairness Across the Network Affordia Health wants partners to succeed.

But growth should not come from damaging another network participant.

Clear rules can govern client attribution, referrals, overlapping markets and cooperation between partners.

When a conflict occurs, the objective should be to protect:

the client first, then the integrity of the network, and then the legitimate commercial interests of the participating partners.

Client First.

This principle applies across the entire Affordia Health network.

From One Referral to Your Own Business You do not need to decide today how large your participation should become.

You can start small.

Refer one client.

Affordia Health provides a structure that can grow with you.

Start with a connection. Build as far as your ambition takes you.

Other Markets The network can continue expanding wherever there is demand for access to healthcare opportunities in China and suitable partners capable of developing client relationships.

Local relationships can originate anywhere. China operations remain connected.

The Network Effect Every partner can bring new clients.

Every completed journey can create operational experience.

More experience can improve our understanding of healthcare providers, departments, locations and practical processes.

Better processes can improve future journeys.

Satisfied clients can create referrals.

Referrals can create new clients.

And successful partners can develop new markets.

The stronger the network becomes, the more useful the infrastructure can become for everyone connected to it.

Referral Program Across the Network The Affordia Health Referral Program provides the simplest entry point into the network.

Where applicable, a qualifying introduction of a new paying Affordia Health client can generate the published referral reward.

More active partners may operate under separate commercial arrangements appropriate to their level of participation.

This keeps the system flexible:

Or you may see an opportunity to build something new.

Who you are Where you operate How you could reach potential clients Whether you want to refer clients or manage your own client relationships Whether you already operate a business Which market you are interested in developing We can then determine which participation model is appropriate.
