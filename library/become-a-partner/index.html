Who Can Become a Partner?

Affordia Health can cooperate with a wide range of providers.

This may include:

Hospitals Specialist clinics Diagnostic centres Rehabilitation providers Traditional Chinese Medicine providers Veterinary hospitals Veterinary surgery centres Specialist veterinary clinics Medical interpreters Transportation providers Accommodation providers Travel service providers Medical technology companies Patient support services Specialist laboratories Recovery and rehabilitation facilities and other providers that can add measurable value to a client journey.

The list is not closed.

If your service can solve a real client need, it may have a place in the Affordia Health network.

Healthcare Is More Than Human Healthcare Affordia Health also supports selected veterinary healthcare journeys.

For example, a client may need:

a specialised veterinary operation, advanced diagnostics, a procedure that is unavailable locally, complex reconstructive surgery, specialist oncology, orthopaedic surgery, or another veterinary service that may only be available at a limited number of institutions.

A veterinary clinic with specialised expertise can become part of our network and make that expertise accessible to international clients.

Specialised capability should not remain invisible simply because the client lives in another country.

Bring Your Expertise Into a Larger Service Network Many excellent providers face the same commercial problem.

They are very good at what they do.

But international clients may never discover them.

Affordia Health can help bridge that gap.

Our network can connect specialist services with clients whose needs match those capabilities.

This can create value for both sides:

Clients gain access to more solutions.

Partners gain access to new demand.

We Do Not Need Every Partner to Offer Everything Affordia Health is built around modularity.

A partner does not need to become a complete healthcare travel provider.

A veterinary clinic can remain a veterinary clinic.

A rehabilitation centre can remain a rehabilitation centre.

A transport provider can remain a transport provider.

A specialist hospital department can remain focused on its medical expertise.

Affordia Health can coordinate these services within the wider journey.

Specialise in what you do best. Connect to a network that handles the rest.

Partner With Affordia Health Without Losing Your Independence Partners can remain independent businesses.

You do not necessarily need to:

change your brand, operate under the Affordia Health name, change your existing business model, or transfer control of your own clients and operations.

The exact cooperation structure depends on the service and partnership agreement.

Our objective is not to absorb every provider into one company.

Our objective is to create a reliable network capable of solving more client needs.

Client Need Comes First Affordia Health does not want to build a directory full of partners simply because they are available.

Partnership should create practical value.

When a client has a specific need, we aim to identify an appropriate solution based on factors such as:

expertise, quality, availability, cost, location, service capability, specialisation, and suitability for the individual case.

A commercial relationship should never automatically determine the solution presented to a client.

Client First.

The partner network exists to increase client options, not reduce them.

More Partners. More Solutions.

A stronger Affordia Health network allows us to solve more complex requests.

One client may need only a hospital appointment.

Another may require:

specialist healthcare, an interpreter, airport transportation, accessible accommodation, rehabilitation, and follow-up support.

Another may be travelling with an animal requiring a rare veterinary procedure.

No single organisation needs to provide every component.

A coordinated network can.

More capabilities create more possible solutions.

Economic Opportunity for Partners International healthcare and specialised care create demand that is often difficult for individual providers to reach.

Affordia Health can help partners access clients beyond their traditional geographic market.

Depending on the partnership structure, cooperation may create opportunities through:

new international clients, additional service volume, access to previously unreachable markets, cross-border referrals, better utilisation of specialist capacity, new revenue streams, and participation in complex multi-service client journeys.

For highly specialised providers, even a relatively small number of appropriately matched international cases can represent meaningful additional business.

We Help Match Capacity With Demand Some providers have specialist capacity.

Some clients have specialist needs.

The problem is often that they never find each other.

Affordia Health helps create that connection.

Our network can identify:

what the client needs and which partner may be able to provide it.

This allows specialised capability to become commercially useful beyond its local market.

From Individual Service to Integrated Journey A partner service can be included as one component of a larger Affordia Health journey.

For example:

Client Need Specialised veterinary surgery unavailable locally ↓ Affordia Health Identifies suitable options and coordinates the journey ↓ Veterinary Partner Provides the specialist procedure ↓ Affordia Health Network Coordinates transportation, accommodation, interpretation and practical support The client receives one coordinated journey while each provider remains responsible for its own area of expertise.

Healthcare Partners Healthcare institutions can cooperate with Affordia Health to improve international patient access.

Potential cooperation may include:

international patient coordination, appointment access, specialist referrals, diagnostic programmes, surgical procedures, rehabilitation, follow-up services, and other agreed healthcare capabilities.

Medical responsibility remains with the healthcare provider.

Veterinary Partners Veterinary providers are an important extension of the Affordia Health model.

We are interested in clinics and hospitals offering:

advanced veterinary surgery, orthopaedics, oncology, neurology, cardiology, reconstructive procedures, advanced imaging, specialist diagnostics, rehabilitation, and other difficult-to-access veterinary services.

International pet owners may be willing to travel when the required expertise is unavailable or economically inaccessible in their home market.

Affordia Health can help make that journey possible.

Travel and Support Partners A successful healthcare journey also depends on reliable non-medical services.

We can cooperate with providers offering:

airport transfers, local transportation, accommodation, interpreting, translation, travel assistance, mobility support, and other practical services.

Quality and reliability matter because these services directly influence the client's overall experience.

Technology and Service Innovation Partners Affordia Health is also open to partnerships that improve how international healthcare journeys operate.

This may include:

healthcare technology, medical travel software, translation technology, patient coordination systems, diagnostic platforms, mobility solutions, AI-enabled support, and other technologies that improve accessibility, efficiency or client experience.

The network should evolve as better solutions become available.

Transparent Partnership Where a commercial relationship with a partner is relevant to a client decision, Affordia Health believes transparency matters.

Clients should understand:

who provides the service, what Affordia Health provides, what the partner provides, and where responsibility lies.

Partnership should make the journey easier, not make the commercial structure harder to understand.

Quality Before Scale We are interested in expanding the network.

But not at the expense of quality.

Affordia Health may evaluate potential partners according to factors such as:

professional capability, service quality, reliability, communication, pricing transparency, client experience, specialisation, operational capacity, and suitability for international clients.

Not every application will automatically result in partnership.

A larger network is useful only when it is also a stronger network.
