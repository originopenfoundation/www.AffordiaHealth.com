Affordia Care Global Online Prevention & Long- Term Health Support You Don't Have to Travel to China to Access Affordia Care.

Affordia Care is a global online service for people who want to take a longer-term approach to their health.

It is designed for people who want to:

prevent health problems where possible, understand potential health risks, explore appropriate non-surgical options, improve their lifestyle, receive access to online consultations with qualified Chinese healthcare professionals, explore Traditional Chinese Medicine where appropriate, or improve their long-term health strategy after treatment or surgery.

You can participate from your own country.

Affordia Care comes to you online.

Health Is More Than Treatment Modern medicine can do extraordinary things.

When surgery is necessary, it can solve problems that could not otherwise be treated effectively.

But treatment of an immediate medical problem and longterm health management are not always the same thing.

A procedure may address a structural or immediate clinical problem while other relevant factors may still deserve attention.

Depending on the condition, these may include:

physical activity, body weight, nutrition, sleep, stress, smoking, alcohol, work environment, physical conditioning, metabolic factors, or other individual risks.

Not every condition is caused by lifestyle and not every recurrence can be prevented.

But where modifiable risk factors exist, understanding them can become an important part of long-term care.

Treat the Problem.

Understand the Risks.

Work on What Can Be Changed.

Prevention Before Problems Become Bigger Healthcare should not begin only when a person needs an operation.

Affordia Care encourages earlier attention.

This may mean:

understanding risk factors, recognising persistent changes, maintaining appropriate preventive examinations, improving everyday habits, seeking professional advice earlier, and monitoring known problems over time.

Prevention cannot eliminate every disease.

But appropriate prevention can help reduce certain health risks and identify some problems earlier.

Earlier understanding can create more options.

Explore Non-Surgical Options Some people are told that they may eventually need a procedure but want to understand whether appropriate conservative options exist first.

Others simply want another professional perspective before making a major decision.

Affordia Care can help coordinate access to qualified professionals who can evaluate available options.

Depending on the condition, appropriate non-surgical management may involve conventional medicine, rehabilitation, physiotherapy, lifestyle modification, monitoring or other professionally recommended approaches.

Traditional Chinese Medicine may also be explored where appropriate.

The objective is not:

“Avoid surgery at all costs.” The objective is:

Understand Your Options Before You Decide.

When surgery is medically necessary, Affordia Care should never be used to delay urgent or necessary treatment.

Online Consultation With Chinese Healthcare Professionals Affordia Care can connect clients internationally with qualified healthcare professionals in China for online consultation where such consultation is appropriate and legally available.

Depending on the client's needs and professional involved, consultations may explore:

existing health concerns, prevention, lifestyle factors, long-term health management, conservative treatment possibilities, Traditional Chinese Medicine, post-treatment support, or another professional perspective.

The healthcare professional — not Affordia Care — remains responsible for their professional medical advice.

Traditional Chinese Medicine China has developed medical traditions over thousands of years alongside its modern healthcare system.

Affordia Care can provide access to qualified Traditional Chinese Medicine professionals for clients who wish to explore this field.

Depending on the professional assessment, TCM approaches may include:

Chinese herbal medicine, acupuncture-related planning, dietary guidance, movement practices, and other traditional approaches.

Chinese herbal medicine is not simply a collection of products to purchase online.

Herbs can have biological effects, side effects and interactions with medications.

For that reason, herbal approaches should be considered with appropriate professional guidance.

Traditional does not mean risk-free. Professional assessment matters.

Before Surgery If you are considering surgery, Affordia Care can help you explore appropriate questions before making your decision.

For example:

Are appropriate conservative options available?

Have relevant lifestyle or physical factors been considered?

Would another professional opinion be useful?

Are there risk factors that could be improved before treatment?

What should be considered during recovery?

The answer may still be surgery.

If so, the purpose of the consultation is not to fight that conclusion.

It is to help you make a better-informed decision.

After Surgery Treatment Can End. Prevention Should Continue.

A successful procedure does not necessarily mean that every relevant long-term risk has disappeared.

Where appropriate, recovery can become the beginning of a new prevention strategy.

Affordia Care can help clients explore:

recovery routines, appropriate physical activity, nutrition, weight management, sleep, stress, rehabilitation, follow-up, and other modifiable factors relevant to their situation.

This is not about replacing postoperative instructions from the treating doctor.

Those instructions come first.

Affordia Care can help build a longer-term strategy around them.

Post-Treatment Reconsultation After an operation or other significant treatment, clients may want to reassess their situation.

What has changed?

What still requires attention?

Which risks remain?

Which habits should change?

What should be monitored?

When should another professional assessment take place?

Affordia Care can coordinate an online reconsultation and help structure the next stage of the client's long-term health journey.

Recovery Is a Phase. Long- Term Health Continues.

Lifestyle Is Not One Product Healthy living has become an enormous commercial industry.

Expensive food.

Supplements.

Vitamins.

Wearables.

Fitness products.

Wellness programmes.

Some may be useful in appropriate circumstances.

But buying one expensive healthy product does not automatically create a healthy life.

Health develops through patterns.

What you repeatedly eat.

How you move.

How you sleep.

How you work.

How you manage stress.

Which risks you expose yourself to.

Whether you smoke.

How much alcohol you consume.

Whether you attend appropriate examinations.

And how consistently you follow professional advice when you have an existing medical problem.

Health Is Built by Patterns, Not Individual Purchases.

An Individual Approach Two people of the same age can have completely different health priorities.

Affordia Care therefore does not begin with a universal lifestyle programme.

It begins with the individual.

Depending on the service, relevant factors may include:

age, health history, current concerns, existing diagnoses, medication, family history, nutrition, physical activity, sleep, work, stress, personal goals, and other relevant circumstances.

Qualified professionals can then determine what information and recommendations are appropriate within their professional scope.

Long-Term Online Cooperation Affordia Care is not intended only for one conversation.

Clients who want continued support may choose a longerterm relationship.

This may include:

Initial Consultation ↓ Individual Health Priorities ↓ Professional Recommendations ↓ Lifestyle Strategy ↓ Monitoring ↓ Follow-Up Consultation ↓ Adjustment ↓ Continued Prevention The frequency and type of professional consultation depends on the individual situation.

Monitor What Matters You do not need to obsess over every number your body produces.

Monitoring should have a purpose.

Where appropriate, qualified professionals may recommend following particular indicators, symptoms, habits or examination results.

The objective is to understand whether the chosen approach is helping and whether something has changed enough to require professional reassessment.

Monitor. Understand.

Adjust.

Mental Wellbeing Long-term health includes mental wellbeing.

Sleep, chronic stress, anxiety, emotional strain and other psychological factors can affect everyday functioning and quality of life.

Affordia Care can help clients access appropriate professional support when needed.

Serious or persistent mental-health concerns should be assessed by appropriately qualified mental-health professionals.

Families and Children Prevention can begin early.

Affordia Care can help families access professional guidance concerning healthy routines, nutrition, physical activity, sleep, development and other appropriate preventive considerations.

We do not promise to make children immune to illness or prevent allergies.

The objective is to support healthy development, recognise concerns appropriately and help families obtain qualified advice when needed.

Prevention at Every Age There is no age at which prevention suddenly becomes irrelevant.

At 20, prevention may mean building good foundations.

At 40, it may mean identifying accumulating risk factors.

At 60, it may mean preserving health, mobility and independence.

At 80, appropriate prevention and monitoring may still contribute to quality of life.

Prevention Has No Expiration Date.

You Don't Need to Be an Affordia Health Travel Client Affordia Care is available as a separate service.

You do not need to:

travel to China, book a Chinese hospital, purchase a medical journey, or undergo treatment abroad.

You can live in Europe, Africa, North America, South America or another supported region and use appropriate Affordia Care services online.

The service is global. The relationship is digital.

And If You Eventually Need More Sometimes prevention and conservative management are enough.

Sometimes they are not.

If further diagnostics, specialist assessment or treatment becomes necessary, Affordia Health can help the client explore additional healthcare options.

Where China becomes appropriate, the wider Affordia Health infrastructure can support:

hospital selection, specialist access, medical coordination, travel, interpretation, local assistance, and the complete medical journey.

Affordia Care therefore does not promise that every problem can be solved online.

It helps you understand when online support is enough — and when the next level of healthcare should begin.

Affordia Care Is Not Emergency Medicine Affordia Care is designed for prevention, long-term support, professional consultation and appropriate exploration of healthcare options.

It is not an emergency medical service.

Urgent or potentially life-threatening symptoms require immediate assessment through appropriate local emergency or healthcare services.

Do not delay urgent medical care while waiting for an Affordia Care consultation.

We do not believe it ends after surgery.

And we do not believe healthy living can be purchased in a single product.

We believe long-term health deserves:

Understanding.

Prevention.

Professional guidance.

Individualisation.

Consistency.

Monitoring.

Adaptation.
