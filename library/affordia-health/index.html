Why China?

China combines a large healthcare system with major tertiary hospitals, highly specialised departments, modern medical infrastructure and access to a broad range of healthcare services.

For international clients, China may offer an alternative when healthcare at home is expensive, difficult to access or involves long waiting periods.

Affordia Health primarily focuses on access to Grade 3A (Tier 3A ) hospitals for hospital-based treatment.

But hospital classification is only the beginning.

Different hospitals and departments have different areas of expertise. We therefore look at the individual case, relevant speciality, available expertise, accessibility, expected timeframe, location and cost.

The goal is not simply to find healthcare in China. It is to find an appropriate option for you.
