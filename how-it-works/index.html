How It Works

Your Medical Journey, Step by Step

Affordia Health makes the process easier by helping you research, organise and coordinate your medical journey in China while keeping you in control of your decisions and expenses.

Free Initial ConsultationExplore Your Options

Start With a Free Consultation

Your journey begins with a free initial consultation. There is no obligation to continue after the initial consultation.

01

1. Start With a Free Consultation

Tell us what you are looking for, your preferred timeframe, your approximate budget, and any other requirements that may be important to you.

02

2. Tell Us What You Need

If you would like to explore your options further, we collect the information necessary to understand your request.

03

3. We Search for Suitable Options

We research suitable healthcare options in China based on your individual requirements.

04

4. You Review Your Options

Where multiple suitable possibilities are available, we aim to help you understand the available options before you decide how to proceed.

05

5. Choose Only the Services You Need

You do not have to purchase a complete medical travel package.

06

6. Clear Service Agreement and Fees

You know what you are purchasing before we proceed.

07

7. Hospital and Treatment Coordination

If you decide to proceed with a healthcare provider, we can help establish communication and coordinate the next steps.

08

8. You Pay for Your Healthcare Directly

Wherever practicable, hospital and treatment costs are paid directly by the client to the healthcare provider.

09

9. Visa and Travel Preparation

You remain the visa applicant and submit your own application.

10

10. Preparing for Your Arrival in China

You should know what happens when you arrive before you board your flight.

11

11. Arrival and Airport Pickup

Having local support from the beginning can make that transition significantly easier.

12

12. Your Healthcare in China

Doctors provide healthcare. We help make the journey around it work.

13

13. Local Support During Your Stay

You are travelling independently - but you do not have to navigate China alone.

14

14. Return Home and Follow-Up

Your journey does not necessarily end when you leave China.
