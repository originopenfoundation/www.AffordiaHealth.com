# Affordia Health - language-market research, evidence ledger

Research date: 14 November 2026. Status: **preliminary, not an approved language ranking**. This report documents what can and cannot currently be inferred; it does not authorize new language pages or assert demand for Shenyang from national totals.

[Liaoning Foreign Affairs Office, hospital appointment list](https://wsb.ln.gov.cn/wsb/wsfw/wjrsfw/2025122514500295967/2025122514500278273.pdf): lists institutions supporting passport-based online appointment services. [Xinhua, 2 March 2026](https://english.news.cn/20260302/2484c2f0f89249f5ac038fc0b0a27583/c.html): describes foreign patients at one Yunnan border hospital, including Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia and Singapore. These sources establish examples of cross-border treatment and local infrastructure. They **do not** substantiate a 30-country demand ranking, medical success comparisons, conversions, company revenue, local language search volume, or an Affordia partnership. State and commercial sources may have promotional incentives; no estimates were invented to fill gaps.

## Language candidates and uncertainty

Candidate inventory: Russian, Arabic, French, Hindi, Bengali, Portuguese, Indonesian, Korean, Japanese, Vietnamese, Thai, Turkish, Urdu, Persian, Uzbek, Kazakh, Mongolian, Malay, Italian, Polish, Dutch and Swahili. Existing Simplified Chinese is retained; Traditional Chinese is a distinct possible future variant, not automatically interchangeable. Brazilian and European Portuguese require separate localization; Arabic requires RTL and locale-aware QA. India requires comparing Hindi and English inquiry intent; Bengali requires separating Bangladesh and India; Central Asian language choices must not assume Russian is everyone's preferred language.

The strongest **leads for further research**, not approved launch decisions, are Russian and Mongolian because source examples directly describe inbound care from Russia and Mongolia. Kazakh/Uzbek merit further research against the Xinjiang example; Arabic/French/Portuguese and others require actual China-specific language-demand and customer-support evidence. Proximity, speaker counts, competitor language switches and traffic elsewhere are not proof of Shenyang conversion. We cannot responsibly assign numerical 0-100 scores to unknown demand, purchasing capacity or support capacity, or identify the most likely first qualified customer without those data. Zero must not be used as a substitute for missing evidence.

### Reproducible follow-up

`node scripts/audit-competitors.mjs ../tmp/pdfs/affordia-competitor-evidence.json` fetched the 15 individual public pages on 14 November 2026 from one network location; all returned HTTP 2xx/3xx in this run. Its JSON snapshot records final URLs, response status, observed HTML `lang` and `hreflang` attributes, observed contact/social links and keyword indicators. Prior text-extraction timeouts were therefore not proof of unavailability. Self-declared `hreflang` is not proof of complete translation, language support, customer acquisition or service outcomes. For example, the sampled HTML listed `en`, `ru` and `ar` on PatientChina, while SinoCareHub's sampled HTML listed `zh-CN`, `en`, `ar-AE`, `ms-MY`, `bn` and `id-ID`; both observations warrant manual verification before competitive conclusions. This automated one-page snapshot does not complete the full competitor, referral or target-country assessment.

## Scoring and release gate

Requested weights: China-relevant demand 25, acquisition potential 20, competitive opportunity 15, purchasing ability 15, geographic/service relevance 10, support capability 10 and feasibility 5. First obtain a dated evidence item and confidence grade for each score; record unknowns separately, never convert them silently to zero. Run an alternative weighting that doubles support capacity and reduces unverified acquisition estimates, then examine ranking changes. The resulting ten languages are provisional until the owner approves both the choice and operational support. Sensitive medical/legal/privacy text requires independent qualified review; do not send patient records to an unapproved translation service.

Planning only: 14 languages at 40 pages each would be 560 URLs, not a mandate to publish thin pages. Existing source has a central locale list but new locale routes, complete reviewed translations, localized metadata, RTL handling where relevant, reciprocal links, support workflow, and content-synchronization gates must be implemented and tested before release. Current actual production remains four languages; no new locales are published by this report.
